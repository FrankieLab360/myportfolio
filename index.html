About me

My name is Francesco Rossitto, a creative freelancer based in Italy. Passionate about bringing ideas to life in every form, I specialize in transforming your visions into tangible realities. My core expertise lies in graphic design (logos), video editing, and 2D/3D animation. I also offer comprehensive support for self-publishing, handling everything from interior layout to book cover design. What sets me apart is my ability to build genuine, friendly connections with my clients. I am deeply committed to understanding your unique needs, responding with enthusiasm, and ensuring your dreams become a reality.

Digital Humanist & Web Developer.
